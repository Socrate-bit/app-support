Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECOM-PARIS LLC ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Tuco mobile application ("the App") or related services.

1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect only the data needed to run the App. We do not ask for your phone number, and we do not access your contacts, photos, or location. You can use the App without creating an identified account: a signed-in account is optional and is only used to keep your progress across devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Voice audio: to score your pronunciation down to the individual sound, and to let you listen back to your own recordings inside the App.
  • Conversation content: to generate the AI tutor's conversational replies, corrections and feedback, and to produce the spoken audio of those replies.
  • Display name: so the AI tutor can address you by name during a call.
  • Learning data: to save your progress, personalize lessons and vocabulary reviews, and display your history and streaks.
  • Anonymous account identifier: to keep your learning data associated with your device across sessions and to let you delete it.
  • Optional sign-in information: to secure your account and restore your progress if you change device.
  • Usage and device data: to understand how the App is used, diagnose bugs, and improve features.
  • Support correspondence: to respond to your requests.
  • All categories: to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Microphone and Voice Data

Tuco uses your device microphone to power AI conversation practice. Microphone access is requested through the standard iOS permission prompt and is used only while you are actively recording an answer.

Speech-to-text runs on your device. Your spoken words are converted into text by Apple's built-in speech recognition, on your device, whenever your language supports on-device recognition. That text — not the audio — is what is sent to the AI service that writes the tutor's replies.

Your voice recording leaves your device in two cases: it is sent to our pronunciation-scoring provider (Section 4) to be scored and is not retained by that provider afterwards; and it is stored in your own account in Firebase Storage so that the App can replay it to you in your call history and pronunciation feedback. You can delete these recordings at any time by deleting your account, as described in Section 10.

We do not collect, store, or retain any biometric or voice-print data, and no biometric identification is performed on your voice.

4. Sharing With Third-Party AI Services

To provide conversation practice, the App sends some of your data to third-party AI services. Before any of this data is sent, and before the microphone permission prompt, the App shows a full-screen notice describing exactly what is sent and to whom, and asks for your explicit consent. If you decline, no data is sent to these services and you can still use the parts of the App that do not require AI — browsing lessons, your vocabulary and your history. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → AI data processing, which immediately blocks all further requests to these services.

Requests to Azure AI Speech and SpeechSuper are routed through our own servers (Google Cloud Functions, hosted in the European Union) so that our API credentials are never shipped inside the App.

5. Other Service Providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Apart from the AI services above, we share data only with the following processors, who act on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Firebase Authentication generates your anonymous account identifier and secures optional sign-in; Cloud Firestore stores your learning data and conversation history; Firebase Storage stores your voice recordings so you can replay them; Cloud Functions run our server-side code. Firebase Privacy Information.
  • Mixpanel (Mixpanel Inc.) — stores usage and device data described in Section 1 so that we can analyze how the App is used and improve it. Mixpanel data is associated with your anonymous account identifier only. Mixpanel Privacy Policy.
  • Superwall (Superwall Inc.) — manages subscription paywalls and purchase state. Superwall Privacy Policy.

None of these providers use your data for their own purposes. All of them are bound by data processing agreements providing protections equivalent to this Privacy Policy. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. International Transfers

Our service providers process data in the United States and other countries. Our own server-side functions run in the European Union; the AI speech services they call may process data in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Data Retention

  • Voice audio: not retained by the AI provider that scores it. A copy is kept in your own account so you can replay it, and is deleted when you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Conversation content and learning data: kept in your profile until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Usage and device data: kept in Mixpanel for up to [24] months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Support correspondence: kept for up to [12] months after resolution.

8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse, including encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest on our providers' infrastructure, access controls limiting who can reach production data, and server-side storage of all AI provider credentials so that they are never shipped inside the App.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR if you are in the EEA or the UK, and under applicable U.S. state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can withdraw your consent to AI processing directly in the App at Settings → Privacy → AI data processing. You may exercise your other rights by using the in-app tools described in Section 10 or by contacting us at the address below. We respond to all verified requests in compliance with applicable privacy laws. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Data Deletion Requests

  • You can delete all your data at any time by deleting your account in the App: go to Settings and tap "Delete account".
  • Deleting your account permanently erases all personal data associated with it (learning progress, vocabulary, conversation history, voice recordings, streaks, and analytics data linked to your anonymous identifier).
  • Alternatively, you can send an email to contact@ecomparis.org with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • All verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days and cannot be undone once completed.

11. Children

Tuco is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a change materially affects how your data is shared, we will notify you in the App and ask for your consent again where required.
