Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECOM-PARIS LLC ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Tuco mobile application ("the App") or related services.

1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect only the data needed to run the App. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials, and we do not access your contacts, photos, or location.

Category What it includes How it is collected
Anonymous account identifier A random user ID generated by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other real-world identity. Created automatically the first time you open the App.
Voice audio Your spoken words during AI conversation practice ("calls"). Captured through your device microphone, only after you grant microphone permission and only while a call is active.
Conversation content Transcripts of what you say, messages you type, the AI's replies, and the language, level, and lesson topic of the current session. Generated during calls and lessons you choose to start.
Learning data Your learning progress, vocabulary lists, lesson and call history, and streaks. Generated by your activity in the App and stored in your profile.
Usage and device data App events (screens viewed, lessons started or completed, calls started or ended, features used), app version, device model, operating system version, language, and country. Collected automatically by the Mixpanel analytics SDK while you use the App.
Support correspondence The content of any email you send us and your email address. Provided by you when you contact us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Voice audio and conversation content: to transcribe your speech, generate the AI's conversational replies and feedback, and produce spoken audio for those replies.
  • Learning data: to save your progress, personalize lessons and vocabulary reviews, and display your history and streaks.
  • Anonymous account identifier: to keep your learning data associated with your device across sessions and to let you delete it.
  • Usage and device data: to understand how the App is used, diagnose bugs, and improve features.
  • Support correspondence: to respond to your requests.
  • All categories: to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Microphone and Voice Data

Tuco uses your device microphone to power AI conversation practice. Microphone access is requested through the standard iOS permission prompt and is used only during active calls. Your voice is processed transiently to transcribe it and to generate a response; audio recordings are not stored by us after processing.

We do not collect, store, or retain any biometric or voice-print data, and no biometric identification is performed on your voice.

4. Sharing With Third-Party AI Services

To provide conversation practice, the App sends some of your data to third-party AI services. Before your first AI conversation, the App shows a notice describing this sharing and asks for your explicit consent. If you decline, no data is sent to these services and you can still use the parts of the App that do not require AI conversation. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings.

Provider What data is sent Purpose Retention by the provider

These providers act as data processors on our behalf. They process data only to deliver the requested service, under written data processing agreements that require them to apply protections at least equivalent to those described in this Privacy Policy. No biometric data is extracted or stored during AI processing.

5. Other Service Providers

We do not sell or rent personal data. Apart from the AI services above, we share data only with the following processors, who act on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Firebase Authentication generates your anonymous account identifier; Cloud Firestore stores your learning data. Firebase Privacy Information.
  • Mixpanel (Mixpanel Inc.) — stores usage and device data described in Section 1 so that we can analyze how the App is used and improve it. Mixpanel data is associated with your anonymous account identifier only. Mixpanel Privacy Policy.

None of these providers use your data for their own purposes. All of them are bound by data processing agreements providing protections equivalent to this Privacy Policy. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. International Transfers

Our service providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Data Retention

  • Voice audio: not stored; processed in real time and discarded.
  • Conversation content and learning data: kept in your profile until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Usage and device data: kept in Mixpanel for up to [24] months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Support correspondence: kept for up to [12] months after resolution.

8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse, including encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest on our providers' infrastructure, and access controls limiting who can reach production data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR if you are in the EEA or the UK, and under applicable U.S. state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may exercise these rights by using the in-app tools described in Section 10 or by contacting us at the address below. We respond to all verified requests in compliance with applicable privacy laws. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Data Deletion Requests

  • You can delete all your data at any time by deleting your account in the App: go to Settings and tap "Delete account".
  • Deleting your account permanently erases all personal data associated with it (learning progress, vocabulary, conversation history, streaks, and analytics data linked to your anonymous identifier).
  • Alternatively, you can send an email to contact@ecomparis.org with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • All verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days and cannot be undone once completed.

11. Children

Tuco is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a change materially affects how your data is shared, we will notify you in the App and ask for your consent again where required.
