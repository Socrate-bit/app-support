Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECOM-PARIS LLC ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Tably mobile application ("the App") or related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account information (a random user ID created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication the first time you open the App).
  • Sign in with Apple information, only if you choose to sign in: the name and email address Apple shares with us (Apple's "Hide My Email" is supported). The App works fully without signing in.
  • Meal-planning preferences you enter during onboarding or in the Preferences tab: first name or nickname, household size, meals per day, cooking days, weekly food budget, country, preferred supermarket, language, cravings, diets, food allergies, preferred proteins, kitchen appliances, and optional survey answers (age range, goals, cooking time).
  • App content stored in your Firestore profile: your recipes, weekly plan settings, shopping list and checked items, favourites, cooked recipes, ratings, and notes.
  • Usage data (app interactions, screens viewed, features used) and basic device information (device model, operating system, app version, and approximate country or city derived from your IP address).
  • Subscription status (whether you have an active subscription or a redeemed referral code). Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment card details.

We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To build your personalised recipes, weekly meal plan, and shopping list.
  • To filter out recipes that conflict with your diets, allergies, protein choices, or available appliances, and to translate recipes into your language.
  • To keep your data in sync across devices when you sign in with Apple.
  • To manage subscriptions, free trials, and referral codes.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve its features and reliability.
  • To respond to user inquiries and customer support requests.
  • To send essential updates and comply with legal obligations.

3. Dietary and Allergy Information

Diets and food allergies you select are used only to choose and filter recipes for you. They are stored in your Firestore profile and sent, without your name or email, to the recipe and AI services described below so that unsuitable recipes can be excluded. We never use this information for advertising and never sell it.

Recipe filtering is automated and may not be perfect. Always check the ingredients of a recipe yourself if you have a food allergy or medical dietary requirement.

4. AI-Processed Data

  • Tably uses Google Gemini (through Firebase AI Logic) to check recipes against your preferences, translate recipes into your language, and merge similar ingredients on your shopping list.
  • Only recipe content and your food preferences (diets, allergies, proteins, appliances, language) are sent to Gemini. Your name, email address, and account identifier are never included.
  • Data sent to Gemini is processed only for the requested task.
  • Recipe searches are performed by our server through the Spoonacular recipe API using only your search filters (such as diets, allergies, ingredients, and cooking time). No personal identifier is sent to Spoonacular.

5. Advertising & Tracking

Tably does not show third-party advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. We do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only as described below:

  • Service providers — We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check) to operate core app functionality, including sign-in and data storage.
  • AI and recipe processing — Google Gemini and Spoonacular process data as described in Section 4.
  • Analytics — Mixpanel receives usage events linked to your anonymous user ID to help us improve the App.
  • Subscriptions — Superwall receives your anonymous user ID, device information, and subscription status to display the paywall and manage access to premium features. Purchases are processed by Apple.
  • Sharing you initiate — If you share your shopping list, it is sent through your device's share sheet to the app or person you choose.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose data where required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

7. Third-Party Services

The following third parties may process data through their SDKs or services integrated into Tably. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google (Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Gemini)
  • Spoonacular (recipe search, accessed through RapidAPI)
  • Mixpanel (product analytics)
  • Superwall (paywall and subscription management)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store payments)

8. Data Retention

We keep your profile and app content for as long as your account exists so that your plan and preferences remain available. When your account is deleted, your personal data is erased within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

9. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect all personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or misuse. Your data in Firestore is only readable and writable by your own account.

10. Children's Privacy

Tably is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us directly. You can also edit your preferences at any time in the App. We respond to all verified requests in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to data portability and the right to object to certain processing.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

14. Data Deletion Requests

Users can request deletion of all personal data associated with their account at any time. To do so, either:

  • Open the App and go to Account > Delete account; or
  • Send an email to contact@ecomparis.org with the subject “Data Deletion Request – Tably”.

All verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days and cannot be undone once completed. Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store subscription; you can cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
